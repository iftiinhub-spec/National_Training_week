HORMUUD UNIVERSITY NATIONAL TRAINING WEEK Training Management System – Functional Summary Theme 2026: Artificial Intelligence for National Transformation Item Description Organizer Hormuud University, Mogadishu Event Type Annual National Training Week 2026 Program 6 days × 3 training sessions per day (18 sessions in total) Audience General public, professionals, university students and developers, and fresh high-school graduates Delivery Primarily virtual, with registration required System Purpose Manage the full training lifecycle from publication and registration through attendance, certification, recordings, and reporting 1. System overview The National Training Week Management System will be the central platform used by Hormuud University to organize and operate the annual National Training Week. The system will bring public information, participant registration, training enrollment, trainer and moderator coordination, online meeting management, invitations, reminders, attendance, feedback, certificates, recorded sessions, and administrative reporting into one platform. Trainers/Speakers are managed as invited training profiles and do not require a system login by default. The platform is designed to support the 2026 Artificial Intelligence program and future editions without rebuilding the system each year. Each annual edition will contain event days, and each day will contain individual training sessions. Participants will register for the sessions they want to attend, administrators will assign a Trainer/Speaker profile and a Moderator to each session, and the Moderator will manage meeting access and operational communication. 2. People who use the system User Main responsibility Public Visitor View the public website: Home, About, Program, Trainings, Training Details, Recordings, Certificate Verification, Contact, Sign In, and Sign Up. Trainer / Speaker Managed Trainer/Speaker profile. No login is required by default; receives the meeting invitation from the Moderator and joins the online session directly. Moderator Authenticated operational user who coordinates assigned sessions, meeting details, Trainer/Speaker and participant invitations, reminders, QR/manual attendance, and attendance updates. Administrator Manage the complete event, users, trainings, assignments, registrations, communications, attendance, certificates, recordings, and reports. 3. Trainer / Speaker • Trainer/Speaker login is not required by default. • Administrator creates and maintains the Trainer/Speaker profile, including name, email, phone, title, organization, biography, photo, and expertise where required. • Administrator assigns the Trainer/Speaker profile to one or more training sessions. • Moderator creates or confirms the meeting details for the assigned training and sends the meeting invitation to the Trainer/Speaker. • Trainer/Speaker receives the invitation by email and joins the online meeting directly using the provided meeting link. • After delivering the session, the Trainer/Speaker can leave the meeting without performing any required action inside the platform. • Optional system access may be enabled later for a Trainer/Speaker who needs a dashboard, materials, schedule, or feedback access, but this is not required for the first production version. • Trainer/Speaker profile information may be shown publicly on training details where Hormuud University approves publication. 6. Attendance Attendance will support both QR check-in and manual entry. The QR process must verify the participant account, confirm registration for the correct training, and prevent duplicate check-ins. The moderator or administrator can review the attendance list and correct an entry when authorized. Each attendance record should keep the participant, training, status, check-in time, attendance method, and the user who made or changed the record. Figure 1. Attendance workflow 9. Certificates Certificates should be issued only after the training is completed and the participant satisfies the approved eligibility rule. The recommended rule is: approved registration, attendance marked Present, and training status Completed. Each certificate should contain the participant name, training title, event name, date, certificate ID, and a verification QR code. Figure 2. Certificate workflow 10. Recorded sessions and feedback • A completed training can have a recording containing the video title, URL, description, thumbnail, related training, and publication status. • Only published recordings appear on the public Recorded Sessions page. • Participants who attended can submit a training evaluation. • Feedback can include content rating, trainer rating, organization rating, comments, and suggestions. • Feedback, attendance records, and other event statistics will support the post-event report. 11. Important operating rules • A participant may register for multiple trainings, but cannot register twice for the same training. • Only published/open trainings can accept public registration. • Trainer/Speaker and Moderator assignments are separate from participant registrations. Trainer/Speaker is normally a managed profile rather than an authenticated system user. • Only authorized Admin or Moderator users can manage attendance. • QR attendance must verify the correct training, participant identity, registration, and duplicate check-in. • Certificates are issued only when the configured eligibility rule is satisfied. • Recordings are normally published after the related training is completed. • Each role can only view or modify information permitted for that role. • Each annual edition remains separate so that future event data does not overwrite previous years. 14. Overall system workflow The platform follows one continuous operational process from event preparation to post-event reporting. The operational sequence is: publish event and trainings, participant registration and approval, create meeting, invite Trainer/Speaker and approved participants, send reminders, deliver the session, record attendance, collect feedback, issue certificates, publish the recording, and complete reporting. This workflow should guide the database structure, user interface, and API design. Figure 3. Overall training lifecycle 15. Development scope The first production version should concentrate on the core National Training Week workflow: event and session publication, participant registration, approval, Trainer/Speaker invitation, meeting communication, attendance, feedback, certificates, recordings, and reporting. Trainer/Speaker login is not required in the first production version. The Participant interface should be a content-first learning/event portal, while Moderator and Administrator interfaces remain operational management dashboards. This functional summary should be approved before database design, interface design, API development, and implementation begin. The proposed structure allows the same platform to support the 2026 event and future annual editions without redesigning the core system.
